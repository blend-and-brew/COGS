Not classified

1Identification

Product name: Shine Ultra Dishwashing Liquid Concentrate Antibacterial

Product type: Concentrated dishwashing detergent

Recommended use: Manual dishwashing and household cleaning.

Manufacturer/brand: Shine / Woolworths

Country of manufacture: Australia

Emergency: Poisons Information Centre — 13 11 26

Described as a concentrated dishwashing liquid with antibacterial action, biodegradable surfactants and grease-cutting properties.

2Hazard identification

GHS classification: Not classified based on available formulation information.

Signal word: None assigned. Pictogram: None assigned.

  • Avoid contact with eyes.
  • May cause eye irritation.
  • Prolonged/repeated contact may irritate skin.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

These precautions are consistent with the product's Australian label information.

3Composition / ingredients

Mixture. The complete formulation and concentrations are not publicly disclosed. Described as containing biodegradable surfactants.

COMPONENTCONCENTRATION

4First aid measures

Eyes: Immediately rinse with running water for at least 15 minutes. Seek medical attention if irritation persists.

Skin: Wash with water. Remove contaminated clothing if necessary. Seek medical attention if irritation/rash develops.

Inhalation: Move to fresh air if irritation occurs.

Swallowed: Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Contact Poisons Information Centre (13 11 26) or a doctor.

5Firefighting measures

Extinguishing media: Water spray, foam, dry chemical or CO₂ as appropriate to surrounding fire.

Specific hazards: Not expected to be readily flammable.

Firefighter protection: Normal protective equipment; self-contained breathing apparatus where required by fire conditions.

6Accidental release measures

  • Avoid eye contact.
  • Contain spill; absorb with suitable material if necessary.
  • Collect and dispose appropriately.
  • Wash remaining residue with water.
  • Prevent large quantities entering waterways or drains.

7Handling and storage

  • Avoid eye contact; wash hands after use.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Keep container closed when not in use.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from excessive heat and direct sunlight.
  • Keep away from food and drink.

8Exposure controls / PPE

Exposure standard: No product-specific standard identified.

Eyes: Safety glasses where splashing is possible.

Hands: Gloves recommended for prolonged occupational handling.

Respiratory: Not normally required.

Ventilation: Normal workplace ventilation adequate.

10Stability and reactivity

Stable under normal storage and use. Avoid excessive heat. Avoid mixing with incompatible cleaning chemicals, particularly strong acids, strong alkalis or oxidising agents. Do not mix with bleach unless specifically directed by the manufacturer.

11Toxicological information

Expected exposure routes: eye contact, skin contact, ingestion. May cause eye irritation and irritation/drying following prolonged skin contact. No product-specific chronic toxicity information was located.

12Ecological information

Marketed as containing biodegradable surfactants and as greywater/septic-tank safe. Avoid uncontrolled release of concentrated product into waterways. No product-specific aquatic toxicity data available.

13Disposal considerations

Dispose of unused product and packaging in accordance with local waste requirements. Do not discharge large quantities of concentrated product to waterways. Empty containers may be recycled where accepted.

The Australian product listing identifies the Citrus Orange formulation and ingredient list. The label states that it is highly flammable.

2Hazard identification

GHS classification (this draft): Flammable Liquid — Category 2; Serious Eye Irritation — Category 2A. Consistent with Australian GHS classification information for ethanol and Australian SDSs for 70% ethanol formulations.

4First aid measures

Eyes: Immediately rinse with water for at least 15 minutes. Remove contact lenses if easy to do. Seek medical attention if irritation continues.

Skin: Wash with water if irritation occurs.

Inhalation: Move to fresh air.

Swallowed: Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting unless instructed by medical personnel. Contact Poisons Information Centre or seek medical advice.

5Firefighting measures

Suitable media: Alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical, CO₂ or water spray.

Hazard: Highly flammable liquid and vapour. Vapour may form flammable mixtures with air.

Firefighter protection: Appropriate protective equipment and self-contained breathing apparatus where necessary.

6Accidental release measures

  • Eliminate ignition sources. No smoking; keep away from flames/sparks.
  • Ventilate area.
  • Prevent entry into drains.
  • Absorb with inert material; collect into a suitable container.
  • Dispose according to local requirements.

7Handling and storage

  • Keep away from heat, sparks and open flames. No smoking. Do not use near ignition sources.
  • Keep container tightly closed.
  • Store in a cool, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep away from children.

8Exposure controls / PPE

Eyes: Safety glasses where splashing is possible.

Hands: Normal use does not normally require gloves; gloves recommended for bulk handling.

Respiratory: Not normally required during intended use.

Ventilation: Good general ventilation.

Ignition control: Essential during bulk handling.

10Stability and reactivity

Stable under normal conditions. Avoid heat, sparks, flames, static discharge and excessive sunlight. Keep away from strong oxidising agents.

11Toxicological information

Eyes: Causes serious eye irritation.

Skin: Frequent use may cause dryness/irritation.

Inhalation: High vapour concentrations may cause irritation or discomfort.

Ingestion: May cause gastrointestinal irritation and alcohol intoxication.

The principal established hazard is the high alcohol content and resulting flammability. Australian HCIS classifies ethanol as a Category 2 flammable liquid and Category 2A eye irritant.

12Ecological information

13Disposal considerations

Confirm final transport classification against the manufacturer's formulation and the current ADG Code before commercial bulk transport.

15Regulatory information

Australian GHS 7 classification applies. Ethanol is listed by Safe Work Australia's HCIS as Flammable liquid Category 2 (H225) and Eye irritation Category 2A (H319).

Source basis

Australian product labels, Australian retailer information, Safe Work Australia guidance and, for Dettol, the manufacturer's Australian SDS. Australia requires GHS 7 for classification and SDS preparation for newly manufactured/imported hazardous chemicals.

Important legal point

The four products should not be treated as one chemical for regulatory purposes. This document is a convenient compilation containing four individual SDSs. If you are the manufacturer, importer, repackager or relabeller of any of these products, this draft should not be represented as the manufacturer's SDS; Australian WHS rules place SDS preparation/classification responsibility on the manufacturer/importer for hazardous chemicals.

Of the four, the Citrus Orange hand sanitiser is the one that should definitely be treated as hazardous/flammable; the available information supports the other three being not classified. The Dettol classification is directly supported by the current Reckitt Benckiser Australian SDS.
